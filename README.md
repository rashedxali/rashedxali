I build in TypeScript with Next.js, Node and Nest.js, and I run the servers underneath. Freelancing since 2021, engineer at [Notionhive](https://notionhive.com/) now, co-founding Adventiq Labs on the side.

The work I like is the unglamorous half: figuring out why a page is slow, where the cache should sit, what happens when a box dies at 2am.

## Things I built and what made them work

### [Gentle Park](https://gentlepark.com/) · e-commerce, Bangladesh and UAE

Reworked the frontend and rebuilt the caching layer. Hosting went from $70 a month to $25, and it now takes ~30M requests and ~150k users monthly without flinching. `Next.js` `Tailwind` `GSAP` `PWA` → [case study](https://www.notion.so/swerashed/Gentle-Park-Leading-E-Commerce-in-Bangladesh-2fbdcf97562480b2ba9bdd3b1ba8cc1f?source=copy_link)

### [Beyond Headlines](https://thebeyondheadlines.com/) · news platform

Server-rendered pages driven by JSON, with load balancing and failover on self-hosted infra. Zero unplanned downtime since launch, which for a news site is the whole job. `Next.js` `SSR` `AWS` → [case study](https://app.notion.com/p/rashedxali/Beyond-Headlines-3d8dcf9756248039ab56d1926c6f5e76?source=copy_link)

### QR Monitor · dynamic QR SaaS

Built it because I wanted QR codes whose destination I could change after printing. Redirect engine plus device and location analytics, bootstrapped to paying users. `Next.js` `Nest.js` `Prisma` `PostgreSQL`

### At [Notionhive](https://notionhive.com/)

Re-architected hosting and caching across the company's sites and cut the monthly bill from $200+ to about $65. Wrote the engineering SOP the Next.js, Node, Nest and CMS teams now work from, and automated enough of the repetitive work to give the team back roughly 20 hours a month.
